## 1. What this day is

No new feature. R3 reviews its own work against the Definition of Done, writes down honestly what is not good enough, and writes what to change. The two documents:

- **docs/progress/R3_frontend/RELEASE_REVIEW.md**: what was delivered per day, each gate with its evidence (yes, partly, no), what is not good enough, the risks, and R3's recommendation (narrow, then a closed beta in one college).
- **docs/progress/R3_frontend/RETROSPECTIVE.md**: what went well, what went badly, what to change, and the next phase in order.

## 2. How to use them at the release meeting

The decision is go, wait or narrow. Gates cannot be voted away: a gate with "no" or "partly" in the review needs either evidence or an explicit written decision by the owner to accept the risk. R3's recommendation is to **narrow**: one college, invite codes, Google sign in, no public Global link, until the three required items (a reviewer, a real phone click-through, production checks) are done.

## 3. What a human must still verify

The review says "no" or "partly" for five things that only people can do: independent review, a real phone, accessibility scan and screen reader, a click-through of Days 5 to 8 on staging, real email sign in.
