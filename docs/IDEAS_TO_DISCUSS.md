# Ideas to discuss before building

Noted 2026-11-13. Nothing here is built. Discuss first, then build in the order at the end.

1. **College, branch and year on the profile.** Mandatory in the tour, shown on the profile. College is typed (with suggestions from known colleges so spellings match). Branch uses the `program` field, year uses `grad_year`. Global stays open to everyone.
2. **Why post and why use the app.** Show the value in the tour and in empty states. Starter prompts so nobody faces a blank box. Come for answers and plans, not for posting.
3. **Anonymous posting.** Hidden from other people, visible to moderators. A steady alias inside one thread. No adult content (minors are allowed). Anonymous posts earn no public credit.
4. **Debates and daily topics.** A daily question, polls with sides, topic spaces people follow. Needs moderation tools first.
5. **Contributions on the profile.** Counts quality (replies others found useful, plans hosted, items returned), not volume. The person can hide them.
6. **Friends and groups.** Follow (one way, no approval) and groups (interest, club, class). Message requests for people you do not follow.
7. **Invites and sharing.** A personal invite link, "invited by", share cards for plans and buzz that look good in WhatsApp.
8. **Challenges.** Weekly, low pressure, curated first. Doing one adds a badge to the profile.
9. **Lost and found, Marketplace and Turf only inside a college.** Hidden in Global, where they mean nothing.
10. **Notifications.** Needed by almost everything above.

## Suggested order
1, 9, 7, 2, 4 (daily question and polls), 3 (limited), 5, 8, 6, groups.
